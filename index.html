SegRPO

Group Relative Policy Optimization with Segment-Level Advantages for Code-Switching TTS

Anonymous Authors

Overview

SegRPO is a reinforcement learning method for code-switching text-to-speech. Each sampled utterance is divided at language switch points, and every segment is scored by its character error rate. Advantages are normalized within the rollout group separately for each segment, so a segment that all rollouts render equally well contributes no update and training concentrates on the segments that differ. All generated samples on this page share the CosyVoice2 backbone, and SegRPO is initialized from the SFT checkpoint.
